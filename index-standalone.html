01 / About

Mathematics across physical and complex systems.

My research connects pure and applied mathematics with astrophysics and particle physics. I am especially interested in the behavior of dynamical systems—from orbital evolution to iteration and fractal structure—and in the numerical methods used to study them.

Across projects involving planets, galaxies, stars, and particle detectors, I combine analytical reasoning with simulations, data analysis, and scientific computing. My mathematical research also investigates fractals and nonlinear dynamical systems.
